Milestones in the deep

Reaching the deepest ocean required generations of exploration technology.

1875

HMS Challenger

Early scientific soundings helped identify extreme depths in the Mariana region.

1960

Trieste

Jacques Piccard and Don Walsh reached Challenger Deep aboard the bathyscaphe Trieste.

TODAY

Modern Exploration
