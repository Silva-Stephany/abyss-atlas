ADAPTATION

Surviving the deep ocean

Life in the deep sea has evolved remarkable strategies for surviving extreme conditions.

✨

Bioluminescence

Organisms produce light through chemical reactions for communication, hunting, defense and reproduction.

◉

Specialized Vision

Many deep-sea animals are adapted to detect blue light, the wavelength that travels farthest through seawater.

⚫

Ultra-dark Camouflage

Some deep-sea fishes have extremely dark skin that absorbs nearly all incoming light, helping them remain hidden from predators.

↓

Pressure Tolerance

Deep-sea organisms possess cellular and biochemical adaptations that allow them to function under enormous pressure.

LIVING LIGHT

Bioluminescence

In the absence of sunlight, many organisms create their own light through chemical reactions.

Blue is the most common color because blue wavelengths travel efficiently through seawater.
